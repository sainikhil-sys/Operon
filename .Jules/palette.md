## 2024-05-18 - Missing focus visibility and aria-labels on icon-only buttons\n**Learning:** Icon-only buttons revealed by hover (`group-hover:opacity-100`) lack keyboard focus visibility and `aria-label`s for screen readers.\n**Action:** Add `focus-visible:opacity-100` along with `group-hover:opacity-100` for keyboard users, and `aria-label`s for icon-only buttons to ensure they're accessible.
